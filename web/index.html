AliExpress Reader

Turn your printed AliExpress orders page into a tidy list: every order, store, item and total, as a spreadsheet or a one-page PDF summary.

…or drop the PDF anywhere on this box.

1Open your orders
On AliExpress (computer, not the app): Account → Orders. Pick View all.
2Load them all
Scroll to the bottom (click View more if it shows) until every order you want is on the page.
3Save as PDF
Press Ctrl+P (⌘+P on a Mac), choose Save as PDF, then open it here.
🔒 Your PDF is read right here in your browser. It is never uploaded, and nothing logs in to your AliExpress account.
Tap an order to see it on your page.

⬇ Export to Xero

Each paid order becomes one bill (Draft) from your supplier, numbered with the AliExpress order number. Cancelled and expired orders are left out. Lines add up to exactly what your card was charged.
CSV: in Xero go to Business → Bills to pay → Import, choose the file, and pick tax inclusive if you chose GST. JSON: the body for POST /api.xro/2.0/Invoices on the Xero API.
